# Why PAYVANTA is not a clone

PAYVANTA does not compete on agent count, LLM theatrics, or chatbot chrome.

It competes on a complete, falsifiable recovery loop.

---

## The moat

1. Detects recoverable revenue as a portfolio.
2. Diagnoses cause from observable evidence — without claiming proven root cause.
3. Generates alternatives, including do-nothing.
4. Compares alternatives on **expected incremental net recovery**.
5. Applies deterministic constraints (budget, consent, cooldown, risk, contact caps).
6. Authorizes safe actions — or blocks them with a recorded reason.
7. Executes idempotently.
8. Measures realized value.
9. Separates **natural** recovery from **incremental** recovery.
10. Emits an immutable Decision Receipt and a sealed 600-cell benchmark.

That loop is the product.

---

## What judges should not see as the headline

- “We have N AI agents”
- Generic reminder / dunning SaaS
- A conversational merchant assistant
- Gross recoveries counted as wins
- Unaudited LLM decisions that move money

Agents exist as orchestration. They are not the pitch.
