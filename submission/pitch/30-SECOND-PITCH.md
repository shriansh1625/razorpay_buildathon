# 30-second pitch (P13)

Merchants lose revenue in fragments — and much of it would come back on its own.

**PAYVANTA** detects revenue at risk, uses Groq GPT-OSS 120B in the sandbox to interpret context and propose recovery candidates, then lets the **deterministic economic engine** price and select the intervention with the best **incremental net recovery** under policy and capacity limits.

AI proposes. Controls authorize. Nothing executes without guardrails and authorization. Every decision leaves a receipt and audit trail.

The Control Room shows one working sandbox batch. The official benchmark evaluates the **same frozen engine** across **600 cells** — not 600 AI runs.

Not a retry engine. Not a chatbot. **Measured. Not claimed.**
